## Update policy
At the start of a task involving fast-moving APIs or libraries, inspect the installed versions and consult the official documentation/release notes for those exact versions. Do not assume a skill's recommendations remain correct forever. Prefer official documentation over blog posts, and record the checked date/version when making a version-sensitive recommendation.
